It has a variety of level hazards, collectables, and player abilities to keep the game interesting, and ontop of having several gamemodes has a page to create your own gamemode, giving customization to just about every variable in the game.

The Offshore Project

This was my submission for the "Offshore Project", a nautical themed community project for Team Fortress 2. The project's goal was to get TF2 modders to create themed content for the game in hopes of working with Valve to make another major update, something Valve has not done since 2017.
My submission was a level and mission for TF2's PvE gamemode, Mann vs. Machine. The level itself is an old repurposed aircraft carrier crashed into an oil rig. The level has several unique enviromental hazards like moving shipping containers which redirect the robots and change where players can take cover, "Storm Waves" which sweep over the main deck of the ship hindering visibility and movement, and explosive oil drums dropped from a helicopter, providing area denial against the players. Custom robots where also added, based around the themes of the enviromental hazards like robots that could create fiery oil spills or walk around unaffected by storm waves. The level was never fully finished as the Offshore Project fell through. The level is currently unpolished but fully working.

Labyrinth V2

This is a Minecraft-themed level designed for a Team Fortress 2 community server. This level has several unique areas, each with lots of secrets, puzzles, and other sorts of interactables. There are four main areas: the Garden, the Underground, the Old World, and the main floor.
The original labyrinth made back in 2021 was smaller, unoptimized, and relatively unpolished so I came back in December of 2025 and made an improved version. The second version has three additional floors, more detail, custom models, scripts, and particle effects. It's also much more optimized, so much so there was enough room to fit the old labyrinth in as the "Old World", a decrepit and dark dungeon floating in the void underneath the rest of the level.

Vampire Hunter

This is a datapack built for Minecraft 1.20.2 which adds my "Vampire Hunter" gamemode, a combat focused, team-based fighting game of humans and vampires. There are custom spells and weapons for the hunters and four unique vampires, being the: Necromancer, Barbarian, Plague Doctor, and Spirit Vampire. Each vampire has a variety of custom abilites ranging from spreading the plague and summoning the dead to smashing through walls and possessing people.

After a long day of gathering food, resources, and ingredients for their spells they prepare for an even longer night ahead. Far to the west, the interior of a dark castle begins to awake as the stone walls shed the last light of the setting sun. Let's Craft a Weapon: 2026

This is a Minecraft-themed level for Left 4 Dead 2. This level is apart of L4D2's campaign gamemode. It is a one chapter campaign where the four survivors must travel to several different dimensions and deactivate generators to beat the final boss and escape. The main gameplay loop involves players traveling through portals to Minecraft's End, Nether, and Aether dimensions where they have to fight through hordes of L4D2's "Special Infected" before reaching and destroying that dimension's generator. The campaign has many custom models, scripts, animations, and particle effects to "Minecraft-ify" the game. The campaign is still in development, but as of now the level is finished, all replacement models for the survivors, infected, weapons, etc. are done, and the first of three cutscenes is finishing animation. This chapter is a prologue to a larger campaign I've been working on.
